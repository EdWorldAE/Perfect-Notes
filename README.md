📖 About

Perfect Notes is an all-in-one Android note-taking and productivity application designed to simplify note-taking, document management, and everyday organization.

Whether you're a student, professional, or someone who enjoys keeping their ideas organized, Perfect Notes brings essential productivity tools together in one application.

✨ Features

- 📝 Note Taking — Create, edit, and manage your notes.
- 📁 Folders — Keep your notes organized.
- 📷 Document Scanner — Scan physical documents using your device's camera.
- 🔤 Text Recognition (OCR) — Extract text from images and scanned documents.
- 📄 PDF Support — Import and export PDF documents.
- 📃 Word Support — Import and export Word documents.
- 🔍 Search — Quickly find your notes.
- 📤 Sharing — Easily share your notes and documents.

📲 Installation

1. Download the latest APK from the Releases section of this repository.
2. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device.
3. Allow installation from the source if prompted.
4. Complete the installation.
5. Launch Perfect Notes and start organizing your ideas.
